About UniNest

Pakistan's most trusted student housing platform, built by students, for students

Our Story

UniNest was born out of a simple yet frustrating experience. As university students ourselves, we struggled to find safe, affordable, and quality accommodation near our campuses. The process was tedious, unreliable, and often led to unpleasant surprises.

In 2024, we decided to change that. UniNest Pakistan was founded with a mission to make student housing search simple, transparent, and stress-free. Today, we are the country's leading student housing platform, serving thousands of students across Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad.

Every listing on our platform is personally verified. We check the property, meet the owners, and ensure that the amenities and conditions match what's advertised. Student safety is our top priority.

Our Mission

To provide every Pakistani student with access to safe, affordable, and high-quality housing near their educational institutions. We aim to eliminate the stress of house hunting so students can focus on what truly matters - their education.

Our Vision

To become the definitive student housing platform across South Asia, expanding to every major university city. We envision a future where no student ever has to compromise on their living conditions due to lack of information or resources.
