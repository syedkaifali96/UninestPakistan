Budget Planning for Student Accommodation

Ye guide Pakistani students ke liye simple aur practical checklist ke sath banayi gayi hai taake hostel, co-living ya private room choose karte waqt confusion kam ho.

Monthly rent limit set karein

Apni total monthly budget ka realistic rent portion decide karein. Rent ke sath utilities, food aur transport ko bhi include karein.

Hidden costs calculate karein

Security deposit, advance rent, laundry, internet, electricity, AC charges, mess fee aur transport expense add karke total monthly cost nikalein.

Meal plan compare karein

Mess included hostel kabhi kabhi cheaper hota hai, lekin quality aur timing check karna zaroori hai. Self-cooking option ho to grocery cost estimate karein.

Emergency buffer rakhein

Har month thora emergency fund side par rakhein for medical, travel, books, repair ya sudden expense.

Simple budget example

Rent PKR 12,000 + meal PKR 5,000 + transport PKR 3,000 + utilities PKR 2,000 + personal PKR 5,000 = approx PKR 27,000/month.
