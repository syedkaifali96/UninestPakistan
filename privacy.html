Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 June 2026

UniNest Pakistan respects student privacy. This page explains how we collect, use and protect information submitted through our website.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone and university.
  • Booking details such as selected property, move-in date, room type and payment reference.
  • Contact, review, newsletter and roommate finder submissions.

How We Use Information

  • To create accounts and manage user dashboards.
  • To save favorites, recent views and booking requests.
  • To contact users about booking status or support requests.

Payment Information

Card CVV is never stored. Payment references are saved only for manual verification unless a real gateway is connected.

Data Protection

Passwords are hashed on the server. Production deployments should use HTTPS, strong JWT secrets and protected admin access.

Contact

For privacy requests, email support@uninest.pk.
